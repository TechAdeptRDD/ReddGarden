      Help & Info

      How to Play: Tap to earn Influence Points. Energy regenerates over time.

      Claiming: Every 1,000 points converts to 1 RDD with a protocol fee of 0%.

      Charity Mode: Choose your donation percentage per claim to fund community grants.

      Transparency: Every economic action is logged in the on-screen ledger and exportable as JSON.

      Staking: Stake from your wallet balance to boost points per tap.

      Prestige: Reset progress at 10,000 points for a permanent multiplier increase.
