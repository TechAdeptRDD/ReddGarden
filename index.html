    Help & Info

    How to Play: Tap the plant to earn Influence Points. Energy regenerates over time, and you can use boosters or stake RDD to increase your earnings.

    Staking: Stake RDD to earn more points per tap. Staking reflects ReddCoin’s Proof-of-Stake Velocity consensus, which rewards both ownership and activity.

    Claiming & Wallet: Every 1 000 Influence Points converts to 1 RDD. Use the Claim button to convert your points and deposit or withdraw RDD using the wallet buttons.

    Referral & Social: Invite friends using your referral link and share on social media to earn bonuses and grow the community.

    Prestige: Once you reach 10 000 points you can Prestige to reset progress for a permanent earnings multiplier.

    Learn & Earn: Use this feature to answer short quizzes about ReddCoin and cryptocurrency. Correct answers grant bonus points!
